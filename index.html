Animal Rescue Program

Our Animal Rescue Program is at the heart of Feline Lifeline’s mission. This program is dedicated to locating, rescuing, and safely transporting cats from harmful or neglectful environments to our facility where they receive immediate care and attention.

Pet Care Program

Our Pet Care Program focuses on providing comprehensive care and rehabilitation to ensure every rescued cat is healthy, happy, and ready for adoption. This program encompasses all aspects of feline well-being, from medical care to behavioral support.

Veterinary Center Program

Our Veterinary Center Program ensures that all cats under our care receive the highest quality medical treatment. The program operates through our on-site veterinary clinic, staffed by experienced veterinarians and technicians.
